02 / TECHNOLOGY

Two phenomena. One precise clean.

01

Thermal shock

The rapid temperature difference makes the contamination brittle and weakens its bond.

02

Sublimation

CO₂ passes directly from solid to gas, expands and lifts contamination away from the surface.

07 / FAQ

Clear before
we begin.

Cost and timing depend on access, size and the type of contamination. An assessment based on a few images is the best place to start.

Is it safe for engines and expensive components?+

Yes, when the parameters are adjusted correctly. Every project is inspected and sensitive areas are protected before work begins.

Do you use water or detergent?+

No. The process uses CO₂ pellets and compressed air. The pellets sublimate and leave no secondary residue.

Does the engine need to be removed?+

Usually not. Removal is recommended only when access or project safety requires it.

How long does it take and what does it cost?+

It depends on complexity, area and contamination level. Send photos or a video and we will return with a realistic estimate.
